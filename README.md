# The Gibbet Apprentice

A dark, grounded browser roleplaying game. You are the apprentice of Corvin, a scrivener and secret necromancer, hanged three days ago at the crossroads outside the plague town of Wexmoor. You have his notes. You have very little else.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step, no server needed.
Use the mouse or the number keys `1`–`9` to choose. Progress autosaves to the browser. Ambient sound is generated in the browser and stays off until you turn it on.

## How it works

- **Time** — each day has Dawn, Day, Dusk and Night. Most actions take one part of the day. Acting at night costs you your sleep.
- **Survival** — eat every day or lose health and will. Sleep restores both. The plague pits can give you fever; grave-moss cures it.
- **Will** — studying, rituals and horror wear it down. At zero you go mad.
- **Corruption** — the cost of necromancy. It makes your spells stronger and leaves less of you behind.
- **Suspicion** — rotting bodies, thralls in the cellar and being seen in the churchyard draw notice. Prayer and honest work help. At 100 the Holy Office comes for you.
- **Necromancy** — get bodies (dig at night, take from the plague pits, or buy from Hesk the gravedigger), then bind them in the cellar as thralls who fight for you. Thralls rot a little every day.
- **Study** — the master's notes teach Grave Chill, Wither, and finally *The Calling Back*.
- **The clock** — the crows are taking a little more of your master every day.

Several endings are possible, depending on what you do with what he left you.
